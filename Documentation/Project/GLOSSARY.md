# Project Glossary

Use these definitions consistently across requirements, architecture, tasks, code documentation, tests, change justifications, and session memory.

| Term                  | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Patient               | A person whose demographic, administrative, and clinical information is maintained by the system.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Patient Record        | The collection of information maintained about a patient, including demographics, encounters, prescriptions, certificates, appointments, and other authorized clinical information.                                                                                      |
| EHR                   | Electronic Health Record. The system and information used to maintain electronic longitudinal health information about patients.                                                                                                                                         |
| EMR                   | Electronic Medical Record. Electronic clinical information associated with care delivered by an organization or provider. This project may colloquially be described as either EHR or EMR, but `EHR` is the preferred project term unless requirements define otherwise. |
| Healthcare Provider   | An authorized clinician who provides healthcare and may create clinical records.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Doctor                | A healthcare provider authorized to perform doctor-specific capabilities such as prescribing or issuing medical certificates where legally permitted.                                                                                                                    |
| Clinic Staff          | An authorized non-clinical or administrative user performing permitted operational functions such as registration, appointment booking, or check-in.                                                                                                                     |
| Administrator         | A user authorized to manage appropriate application, clinic, user, role, or permission configuration.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Consultation          | The clinical interaction between a patient and healthcare provider.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Encounter             | The system representation of a healthcare interaction. A consultation is initially represented as a type of clinical encounter.                                                                                                                                          |
| Consultation Note     | Clinical documentation recorded by a healthcare provider during or after a consultation.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Clinical Record       | Information documenting clinical care, observations, decisions, treatments, documents, or other healthcare activity associated with a patient.                                                                                                                           |
| Patient History       | The authorized chronological or categorized view of historical patient information maintained by the application.                                                                                                                                                        |
| Prescription          | A clinical document/instruction issued by an authorized prescriber specifying medication and directions for a patient.                                                                                                                                                   |
| Prescription Item     | An individual medication entry contained within a prescription.                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Medical Certificate   | A clinical document issued by an authorized provider certifying information permitted by applicable clinical and legal rules.                                                                                                                                            |
| Appointment           | A scheduled interaction between a patient and healthcare provider/clinic at a specified date and time.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Booking               | The process of creating or reserving an appointment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Check-in              | The process of recording that a patient has arrived for a scheduled appointment or supported clinic visit.                                                                                                                                                               |
| Appointment Status    | The current administrative state of an appointment, such as booked, checked-in, completed, cancelled, or another approved state.                                                                                                                                         |
| Laboratory Request    | A request for one or more laboratory/pathology investigations. This is initially a future capability.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Laboratory Result     | Clinical information returned from a laboratory following requested testing. This is initially a future capability.                                                                                                                                                      |
| Clinical Document     | A structured or printable artifact associated with clinical care, including prescriptions and medical certificates.                                                                                                                                                      |
| Print-ready Document  | A deterministic representation of a clinical document suitable for printing or export, independent from its editing interface.                                                                                                                                           |
| Finalized Record      | A clinical record that has reached an approved state in which modification may require an amendment or other auditable process rather than unrestricted editing.                                                                                                         |
| Amendment             | A traceable correction or addition to an existing finalized record that preserves appropriate historical information.                                                                                                                                                    |
| Audit Event           | A record indicating that a significant action occurred, including the responsible actor, action, target, date/time, and other relevant context.                                                                                                                          |
| Role                  | A named collection or category of authorization capabilities assigned to a user.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Permission            | Authorization to perform a specific operation or access a specific resource.                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Authentication        | The process of establishing the identity of a user.                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Authorization         | The process of determining whether an authenticated user may perform an action or access information.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Domain                | Core business concepts and rules that do not depend on delivery frameworks, databases, UI libraries, or infrastructure.                                                                                                                                                  |
| Use Case              | Application-level operation coordinating domain behavior to accomplish a user or system goal.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Clean Architecture    | Architecture in which source-code dependencies point toward inner business/application layers and infrastructure/framework concerns remain outside the core business rules.                                                                                              |
| Repository            | The source-control repository containing the project.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Monorepo              | A repository containing multiple applications and reusable packages managed together.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Shared/Common Library | A package containing genuinely reusable TypeScript code needed across multiple applications or architectural boundaries. It must not contain frontend- or backend-specific implementation merely for convenience.                                                        |
| GraphQL Schema        | The externally exposed GraphQL type/query/mutation/subscription contract.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Resolver              | An interface-adapter function connecting a GraphQL operation/field with the application's use cases or mapping logic.                                                                                                                                                    |
| Domain Entity         | A domain object with identity and business behavior/rules.                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Value Object          | A domain object defined primarily by its value rather than independent identity.                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Persistence Model     | Database-oriented representation used to persist data. A Prisma model is not automatically a domain entity.                                                                                                                                                              |
| Requirement           | An approved statement of required product/system behavior or constraint.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Acceptance Criterion  | A verifiable condition that must be satisfied for a requirement/task to be considered successful.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Finding               | An observed issue, gap, risk, or opportunity identified during assessment that has not automatically been authorized for implementation.                                                                                                                                 |
| Task                  | A bounded unit of work derived from approved project needs.                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Change Justification  | The required record documenting what is changing, why it is changing, its requirement source, test impact, approvals, and verification evidence.                                                                                                                         |
| Jira Ticket           | An externally managed Jira work item that may act as the source of a requirement or task when explicitly selected as such.                                                                                                                                               |
| Task Branch           | A Git branch associated with one approved task/Jira ticket.                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Verification          | Evidence that implemented behavior satisfies relevant acceptance criteria and has passed required automated/manual checks.                                                                                                                                               |

## Initialization clarifications — 2026-09-10
- Draft requirement: a versioned proposal for intended behavior; it is not an approved requirement or task.
- Proposed task: local bounded work awaiting explicit scope approval; no Jira ticket is used. Earlier Jira/external-ID examples are inactive vocabulary.
- Issued document: a versioned clinical content snapshot whose permitted lifecycle and legal meaning require owner decisions; rendering or printing does not itself constitute issuance.
- Print event: evidence of an application render/print request or observed adapter result, not proof paper was physically printed.
- Clinic scope: an authorization boundary to be decided; it does not imply approved multi-tenant SaaS behavior.
